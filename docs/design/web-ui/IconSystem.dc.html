Verdict icons — how the set composes

Three major states × an optional minor badge. Four metaphors for the major glyph; one shared rule for everything else.

The rules
Major carries colour and form

Green / amber / red from the existing --cw-* ramp, but the three states are also three different shapes. The greyscale row on each direction is the test: if you cannot tell them apart there, the icon fails for eight percent of men and every monochrome tray.

Minor reuses your own law

From encoding.ts: dot = one site, bar = shared path, warm = out there. So the badge is a warm dot for a site incident and a warm bar for internet-wide trouble — the same marks already used in the ledger and punchcard. Nothing new to learn.

The badge has a floor

It is legible down to about 20 px and no further. Below that, drop it and show the major state alone — at tab and tray size the major answer is the one anyone wants. Minor is also only observable while the link is up or struggling: with the link down, nothing beyond the modem can be measured.
